## Welcome to my GitHub Page

This site showcases some projects like a Movie Watchlist, Medtracker, and a Clinical Timetable app. Both use Airtable as a simple backend. The timetable app is a progressive web app (PWA) and can be installed on iOS or Android. Currently it shows a list of daily events in a clinical setting in Germany but it can be adapted to display other events. It is available in two locales, English and German. It is possible to mark events as cancelled and drag and drop the events into a new order. The Movie Watchlist is a place to keep all those movie recommendations that you get from friends and on top of that store them in a list where they can be marked as the next movie to watch or marked as watched. This is also a PWA so it can be installed on an iOS or Android homescreen. You can search by movie name or a cast member and the new movie will populate the rest of the fields automatically. Medtracker helps you keep track of your medication, when you're supposed to take them: morning, midday, evening, night. In addition you can score yourday and say how good you're feeling from 1 to 10 and track side effects from the medications. All apps were created with the help of Claude AI.
